### C. Water and atmosphere

- **Shoreline masking:** straight `fillRect(0, seaHorizonY, W, H-seaHorizonY)` mask (`cinderholm-env.ts:224-225`) + 2 procedural foam rects (`cinderholm-env.ts:227-231`) is a placeholder, not a shoreline. The foam lip softens the cut at thumbnail scale only; at full res the seam across the cliff is unmistakable.
- **Wave/foam:** sea whitecaps suit open sea; foreground rocks carry baked break-foam; the two never meet at the castle cliff (missing contact foam). Scale mismatch: foreground foam is large/broken, sea texture is fine swell — needs an intermediate foam treatment at the cliff foot.
- **Ocean movement:** static plate + foam alpha pulse is calm-acceptable, but the sea reads frozen next to the animated keeper/vessel. No scroll/wash. Unused assets already staged for this: `previews/assets/water-sheen-landscape.png`, `water-sheen-portrait.png`, `mist-landscape.png` — decision is whether to approve them.
- **Fog/depth:** mountains carry baked mist; castle is crisp against them and pops forward too hard. A light aerial tint / mist strip at the castle–mountain junction would seat it.
- **Lighting:** warm lantern/beacon vs cool storm is the best color story in frame. Missing: zero spill — no beacon sweep, no lantern glow on water, no window reflection lane. `previews/assets/beacon-beam-landscape.png` / `-portrait.png` exist but are unapproved/unused.

### D. Portrait composition

Center-crop reframe fails and must not be iterated by further cropping:

- Keeper (430,1595) floats — feet over the water gap, no rock beneath at this crop.
- Vessel (705,1346) is cramped against the stair base; hull nearly touches the keeper's vertical lane.
- Castle is left-truncated (gatehouse/left bastion cut), lantern tower dominates the top third, right bastion wall cut.
- Rocks crowd the center; the inlet lane that reads clearly in landscape becomes a narrow slot.
- Conclusion: portrait needs **independent staging** (own waterline, own keeper/vessel coordinates, possibly own plate crops) — not a crop of the landscape frame. The current `STAGE.portrait` (`cinderholm-env.ts:57-61`) proves restaging alone cannot fix grounding because the rock geometry under the crop has no platform at x~430.

## 3. Landscape art-direction recommendations

1. Keep the 5-plane stack and the rock-framed inlet — composition skeleton is approved as-is.
2. Fix the waterline with a shaped mask + contact foam (E1) before any other landscape work.
3. Move the vessel off the stairs into the inlet lane (see §6) and sink the hull to a draft line.
4. Restage the keeper onto the wider shelf (E3+E8 combined move).
5. Seat the castle with a mist strip at the mountain junction (E4); defer any repaint.
6. Optional motion pass: sheen drift (E5); beacon spill only with explicit approval (E6).

## 4. Portrait art-direction recommendations

1. Abandon center-crop reframing. Author portrait as its own layout.
2. Proposed starting point (to be validated in-preview, not integrated): waterline lower relative to frame than y1226 equivalent, keeper moved onto a visible rock shelf with full boot contact + contact shadow, vessel pulled right/down clear of the stairs and clear of the keeper's vertical lane, castle framed to keep gatehouse + lantern (slight right shift of the castle layer or a dedicated portrait castle crop).
3. Accept that portrait may need its own castle/rocks crops as derivatives (§8) — decide after one restage trial whether constants alone suffice.

## 5. Waterline treatment recommendation

**Shaped shoreline mask + contact foam collar (E1).** The straight rect mask is the defect; procedural foam rects cannot fix a geometric problem. One hand-authored shoreline alpha (following the cliff foot, dipping at the inlet) plus a foam collar asset where water meets cliff and hull will resolve landscape and portrait simultaneously. No P1 plate is repainted; the mask is a new derivative asset applied at integration.

## 6. Vessel positioning recommendation

Moored-in-inlet, clear of architecture: place the hull fully inside the tidal channel, bow pointing toward the inlet mouth (right of frame in landscape), with at least one hull-length of clear water between stern and stair base, mast clear of the tower doorway, and hull sunk to a plausible draft with a foam collar. This reads as "at anchor in the cove" instead of "beached on the steps" and unblocks the stair read. Exact pixels to be locked in-preview during production staging — do not hard-code here.

## 7. Final proposed Cinderholm composition

Storm sky full-bleed; misty far peaks seated behind with a mist strip softening the left roof junction; castle headland midground with lit windows and lantern as the warm anchor; shaped shoreline with contact foam where cliff meets sea; open-swell sea confined to the inlet lane with faint sheen drift; foreground rocks framing the lane with baked break-foam; keeper grounded on the wide west shelf with boot shadow, lantern lit, silhouette clear of the backsplash core; gloam vessel at anchor in the channel with draft + foam collar, clear of stairs and doorway; beacon sweep and window-glow lane as a restrained optional grade. Portrait is the same story re-staged natively (keeper grounded on a visible shelf, vessel in open water of the slot, castle complete), not a crop.
